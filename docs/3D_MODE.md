# Mr. White — 3D Mode (Phone Play)

## What is 3D Mode?

**3D mode** lets you play Mr. White on any device with a web browser — no VR headset required. You can use a phone, tablet, or desktop computer to sit at the same table as players wearing VR headsets or other phones. The room is rendered in full 3D, and you control your view by dragging to look around.

> **Same game, same rules, same tables.** Your devices don't matter. A phone and a headset share one table; VR players see the same room you do, just from a different display format.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Open the Page

Navigate to the VR version of Mr. White in your browser. You'll land on the entry screen with two options:

- **Start a table** — create a new game and invite others with a four-letter code
- **Join one** — enter a four-letter code to join a table someone else started

### 2. Pick a Name

Type your name (up to 16 characters). This name appears to everyone at the table when you give hints or make guesses.

### 3. Start or Join

Click the button. The room will load and you'll be seated at the table. The overlay will disappear and you'll see the villa with your view centered on the table.

## How to Play

### Look Around — Mouse or Drag

Drag your finger (or mouse) across the screen to rotate your view. Look up, down, left, and right to see:

- The **status panel** above the table (who's turn it is, phase of the round)
- The **word card** to your left (your word or role)
- The **log panel** to your right (what's been said so far)
- Other **players around the table** (their names and status)
- The **keyboard** in front of you (for typing hints and guesses)
- The **action button** (to deal rounds or reveal the answer)
- The **Leave button** (to leave the table)

### Tap to Press

When playing on a phone or without a VR headset, a single tap (not a drag) will press whatever is under your finger:

- **Press a keyboard key** — type a letter or backspace
- **Press the action button** — deal a round, end a round, or reveal the answer
- **Press the leave button** — exit the table and return to the entry screen
- **Press the three-dot menu** (if playing in a headset) — access calibration

### Text Entry

When it's your turn:

- **To give a hint** (civilians only) — type one word that hints at the secret word, then press ✓ or press Enter on your physical keyboard
- **To make a guess** (after being caught) — type your guess at what the secret word is, up to 30 characters, then press ✓ or Enter

Your physical keyboard always takes priority if one is available.

## Game Phases

### Lobby

Everyone is gathered but the round hasn't started. The host (first player at the table) sees a **Deal the round** button. Everyone else waits.

**Your action:** Wait for the host to deal, or leave the table.

### Hint Phase

The hinter (one player) is giving a one-word hint. Everyone else waits to hear it.

**Your action:** If you're the hinter, type your word. Otherwise, wait and read the hint in the log.

### Vote Phase

Everyone votes to eliminate a player (who they think is Mr. White).

**Your action:** Tap on a player's name to vote for them. Each player can only vote once per round.

### Guess Phase (If Caught)

The player who received the most votes is being asked one final question: what is the secret word?

**Your action:** If you were caught, type your guess. Everyone else waits.

### Reveal Phase

The answer is shown and the round result is displayed.

**Your action:** Read the result or deal the next round (if you're the host).

## Controller Support

Mr. White supports **gamepad controllers** (Xbox, PlayStation DualSense, etc.) so you can play from the couch without a VR headset.

### Supported Buttons

When a gamepad is connected, you can:

- **Press any button** — completes calibration (confirms the button works)
- **Press the right trigger** — in 3D mode, this acts like a tap at the center of your screen to press the nearest button

### Calibration

**Calibration** confirms that your controller works with the game.

#### How to Calibrate

1. **Open the menu** — tap the **three dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner of the screen
2. **Select "Calibrate controller"**
3. The game will display: *"Press the screen, a gamepad button, or a VR trigger to calibrate"*
4. **Press any input:** tap the screen, press any gamepad button, or squeeze a VR trigger
5. The game confirms: *"Calibrated — that input presses in VR"*

> **Tip:** If you connect a gamepad, calibration starts automatically. Just press any button or the screen to confirm it works.

#### How Calibration Works

- **In 3D mode:** Calibration sets up your controller for when you enter VR
- **In VR mode:** You can calibrate without removing the headset by using the small three-dot button floating in the room (next to the Leave button)
- **Calibration is persistent:** You only need to calibrate once per device; the setting is saved

## Tips for 3D Mode Play

### On a Phone

- **Use landscape orientation** for a wider view of the room
- **Tap a player's face to vote** — tapping their name area votes for them
- **Buttons are larger** (30% bigger) to hit easily with a finger
- **Button backgrounds are semi-transparent** so you can see through them and read the room behind

### With a Gamepad Controller

- **Connect before joining the table** for calibration to start automatically
- **The right trigger acts like a center-screen tap** in 3D mode, so you can select buttons without looking at the screen
- **You can still use the screen** — both input methods work together

### On a Desktop or Tablet

- **Drag with the mouse** to look around
- **Single-click to press** any button or key
- **Physical keyboard always works** — type directly when the keyboard is visible

### Reading the Room

- **Status panel** tells you whose turn it is
- **Word card** appears when you're playing and the word has been dealt
- **Log panel** shows the full history of the round
- **Other players' faces** appear around the table; look at them to see their expressions

## Common Issues

### "No headset detected" / "No VR in this browser"

You're not using VR — that's okay! 3D mode works on any device. This message just means WebXR isn't available, which is normal on phones and desktop browsers without a headset connected.

### Gamepad isn't working

1. **Reconnect it.** Disconnect and reconnect the controller; calibration should start automatically.
2. **Tap the screen or press a button to calibrate.** The game needs to confirm the input works.
3. **Check your browser.** Most modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari 16+) support gamepads. Older versions might not.

### Can't tap a button or keyboard key

1. **Try dragging less.** If you move more than 3 pixels while pressing, it counts as a drag (for looking), not a tap.
2. **Make sure you're not in VR mode.** In immersive VR, you need a hand-held controller or the in-world buttons.
3. **Check the overlay.** If the entry screen is still open, you can't interact with the game yet.

### Text entry is slow or unresponsive

- **Use a physical keyboard** if you have one — it's faster and more reliable than the on-screen one.
- **Press Enter** to submit instead of tapping the checkmark button.
- **The keyboard hides automatically** when it's not your turn, so you're not covering the game.

## Joining a Friend's Table

1. Ask your friend for the **table code** (a four-letter code like `ABC1`)
2. Go to the entry screen and paste or type the code in the **"or join one"** field
3. Type your name and press **Join**
4. You'll be seated at the same table

> **Pro tip:** The table code appears in the URL. You can share the full link and it will auto-fill the code.

## Playing Alongside Headset Users

The same game runs on headsets, phones, and desktops. You can:

- Join a table started by someone in a VR headset
- Have headset and phone players at the same table
- All see the same room, same players, same game state

The only difference is *how* you see it:

- **Headset:** stereoscopic 3D, head-tracked view, hand controllers
- **Phone/Desktop in 3D mode:** flat screen, mouse/touch input, gamepad support

The rules, the word, the votes — everything is the same.

## What If You Have Both a Headset and a Phone?

You can switch between them:

1. **Start on your phone** in 3D mode to set up the table
2. **Enter VR** by putting on the headset (press the "Enter VR" button)
3. **Return to 3D mode** by taking off the headset

Your seat at the table stays the same. Other players won't know you switched — they just see you at the table.
