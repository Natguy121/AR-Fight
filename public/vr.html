Round table, cosy villa, a keyboard you type on in mid air. The same tables as the phone game — headsets and phones can sit at one together.

or join one

Got one of the cardboard or plastic viewers you drop a phone into? Sit down here first, then tap Phone in a viewer — the screen splits into two lenses, your head steers, and you pick things by looking at them. No viewer and no headset? Drag to look around and click to play; the table works exactly the same.
