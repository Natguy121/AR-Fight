Mr. White

Round table, cosy villa, a keyboard you type on in mid air. The same tables as the phone game — headsets and phones can sit at one together.

or join one

Got one of the cardboard or plastic viewers you drop a phone into? Sit down here first, then tap Phone in a viewer — the screen splits into two lenses, your head steers, and you pick things by looking at them. If your phone won't rotate to landscape on its own — rotation lock, usually — it draws the two lenses turned to fit anyway, no action needed; tap Flip in the seam between them if it looks turned the wrong way. No viewer and no headset? Drag to look around and click to play; the table works exactly the same.
