How it works
  1. Everyone is shown the same secret word — except Mr. White, who is shown nothing and does not know the word at all.
  2. Going round in turn, each player says one word that hints at the secret word without giving it away.
  3. Mr. White has to invent a hint that fits, from nothing but what the others have already said, and work out the word as they go.
  4. Then everyone votes. Whoever gets the most votes is out, and their role is revealed.
  5. If Mr. White is caught they get one guess at the word — and if they name it, they win the round anyway.
  6. Civilians win by catching every Mr. White. Mr. White wins by surviving until the table is level.

The first player to speak is never Mr. White — with no word and no hints yet, that seat would be unplayable.
