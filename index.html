Remade

Look at your room and watch it become something else — while staying exactly where it is.

It works out what each thing in front of you actually is, then gives each one its own material: the TV becomes a whiteboard, the sofa a gaming couch, the chair charred wood, the walls bare plaster. Nothing moves — every object keeps its own shape, its own shading and its own place, so you can still reach out and pick it up.

Leave this on if you are slotting the phone into a Cardboard/VR shell. Turn it off to hold the phone like a window.

Needs HTTPS (or localhost) for camera access. Tracking runs on the device; a frame leaves it only if you connect Claude yourself.

Remade is built for a phone on its side — the same way it sits in a headset shell.

Still showing sideways after you've turned the phone? Check that rotation lock is off — the round‑padlock icon in iOS Control Centre, or the auto‑rotate toggle in Android's quick settings. With it on, the screen can't turn no matter which way the phone is held.
