AR‑Fight

Draw a weapon in the air. Tell it what it is. Then use it.

  1. Draw — pinch thumb and index together and move your hand.
  2. Classify — is it a gun or a melee weapon?
  3. Tag — point at your drawing to mark the muzzle, trigger and grip — or the grip and striking edge.
  4. Fight — it snaps to your hand and works.

Leave this on if you are slotting the phone into a Cardboard/VR shell. Turn it off to hold the phone like a window.

Needs HTTPS (or localhost) for camera access. Nothing leaves the device — all tracking runs locally.
